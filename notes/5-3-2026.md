The loosely related groups of artisans that tend to be along rivers and headwaters at the edges of the world. 
These include groups and villages like The Consortium Of Herbs in Everthistle, Lampwell - a village of leatherworkers in high meadows, Sendil - a village of woodworkers and arborists, Hinul - a guild and village known for cultivating spices and special crops growable in a crystal desert.
