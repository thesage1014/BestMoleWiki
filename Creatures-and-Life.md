# Creatures and Life
## The speaking races
There's a major threshold between two types of creatures of the world: that of the creatures who can speak to one another, and those who can't. With so many intelligent species, the hard distinciton is made between 'animals' and people based around weather or not they speak. The divide isn't just social, but a fundamental part of how the mind works.

## The feywild
All natural things have some presence in the feywild. The feywild isn't exactly a place, as it's basically the forum of all natural things in constant communication. Much like the astral plane, things in the feywild are largely metaphors of things in the material plane. You can have very tangible and valid experiences in these places, but they exist in massive superposition from the point of view of the material world.  
The intelligent races, in their fixation of sculpting their reality, lost their connection to the feywild and generally can't tap into this. They appear as wisps, bursting around in intermittent movement, throughout the skies of the feywild.

## Energy
Many animals of the universe can absorb light from the sun. Notably all birds get their energy this way. They still need to eat for the odd nutrients, but their energy and ability for long distance travel is based on their ability to absorb sunlight.
