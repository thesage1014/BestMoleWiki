# Magic's place in the universe
## A Wizard
Learning the ways of magic is a dangerous endeavor. A wizard is one who who has travelled to the astral plane and learned there is no spoon. It's easy to lose one's mind in training to be a wizard, as the way of learning is to let go of one's connection to their body and mind as much as possible, but leaving just enough connection to find their way back with new knowledge. The first lessons of magic always involve learning that you and everything else are all parts of the same thing, of the same unified universe.

## The Astral Plane and Quantrite
Quantrite has its own language in a way. When exploring the astral plane, a wizard or shaman learns and finds the high level forces and primordial intentions of quantrite stabilization.
